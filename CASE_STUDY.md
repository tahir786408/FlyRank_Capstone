# Case Study: Settings Form — Prompting Precisely vs. Prompting Vaguely

**The Problem**
I needed to build a settings form where users could change their password and notification preferences. I wanted to test something: does *how* you prompt AI actually change the quality of what it builds? So I built the same form twice — once with a vague prompt, once with a precise one — and compared the results.

**What I Did**
Round 1, I gave AI a vague prompt — just "build a settings form." The output was basic: no validation, and it threw errors when I tested it.

Round 2, I got specific. I told AI exactly which fields I needed, what validation rules to apply, and what the form should check for before submitting.

**What Came of It**
The second version had proper validation and worked correctly. Same tool, same task — the only thing that changed was how clearly I asked. That's the real lesson: AI doesn't replace decisions, it executes them. The quality of the output is a direct function of the quality of the input.
