# The Through-Line: Content Map & CTAs

## One-Line Claim
> "I don't just prompt AI to write code — I know when to trust it and when to fix it."

## The One Action (from Week 1)
Get a startup founder/CTO to message for an interview.

## Content Map

### Home Page
1. **Hero** — One-line claim + short subhead explaining who this is for (startup founders hiring junior full-stack devs)
2. **Proof strip** — 2-3 quick stats or logos-of-tools-used (Claude, Cursor, React, Next.js)
3. **Featured case study** — the strongest one (Weather App or the Registration Form debugging case), leading with the most impressive result first
4. **CTA: "See how I work"** → links to Case Studies page

### Case Studies Page
1. **Intro line** — reinforces the one-line claim
2. **Case 1: Registration Form (Prompting Fundamentals)** — the problem, what I did, what came of it; this leads because it best proves "know when to fix it" (10 manual bug fixes across 5 prompt iterations)
3. **Case 2: Weather App** — shows working end-to-end from scratch, including real debugging (env file bug, scoping bug)
4. **Case 3: Login Intro Animation (Prompt Ladder)** — shows iterative judgment across 5 versions and two AI models
5. **CTA: "Let's talk about your project"** → links to Contact section

### About/Contact Page
1. **Short bio** — who I am, what I'm learning, what I'm building (StudyBuddy capstone)
2. **The identity kit in action** — link to the live StudyBuddy app as a "see it live" proof point
3. **CTA: "Message me for an interview"** — email + LinkedIn, the final action every other CTA ladders up to
